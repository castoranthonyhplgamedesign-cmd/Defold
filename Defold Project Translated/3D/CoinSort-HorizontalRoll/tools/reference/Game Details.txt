A horizontally-oriented hollow cylinder-shaped level of 12 columns and multiple rows of 2D stacked coins of different colors that is rotateable and moveable - with another horizontally-oriented hollow cylinder-shaped level of 6 columns with the same rows of 2D stacked coins of different colors inside of it that is unrotateable and unmoveable, purely cosmetic.

The columns are made up same colored coin row segments composed of a defined number of coin rows. Since the level is horizontally-oriented, the row segments that line up can be rotated around the cylinder level like a revolver.

If an entire column has matching same-color row segments on rotation release, that column clears and triggers an animation of coins sequentially flying out. These coins will fill up a coin jar somewhere in the level.

What I described above is basically the entire mechanics of the game.

Now here's the level setup:
Three horizontally-oriented hollow cylinder-shaped levels of differing construction, all lined up vertically from top to bottom. First level is at the top.

1st Level:
12 columns and 2 row segments. Clear objective: match 2 columns to clear (doesn't need to empty out the level)

2nd Level:
12 columns and 3 row segments. Clear objective: match 3 columns to clear (doesn't need to empty out the level)

3rd Level:
12 columns and 4 row segments. Clear objective: none (on tap of this level, we zoom out then show the fade-in end card).

Only one level is unlocked at a time. To show all levels at the start, the camera is zoomed out and will zoom in to the first level on tap. Camera will pan down to the next level that is unlocked after clearing the current level (all animations finished, and clear objective accomplished). The locked levels colors are darkened out with a white padlock image in the middle of the level.
