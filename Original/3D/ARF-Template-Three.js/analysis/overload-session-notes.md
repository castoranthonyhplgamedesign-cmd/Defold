# Conveyor overload — session notes (2026-10-28/29)

Decisions and current state from the tuning session. Evidence level: automated tests (114 pass), typecheck,
and the local Chromium smoke `tools/white-preload-smoke.mjs`. Physical devices: unverified.

## Level
- Playable level: `overload-level.arrow-flow.json` = arrows + conveyor from `conveyor_overload_v2.arrow-flow.json`
  plus the three cannons of `untitled-level.arrow-flow.json`, moved +0.798 in y to follow the v2 conveyor shift.
  Both source files are unchanged. Re-exporting v2 means merging the cannons again (done by hand; no script).
- v2: 60 arrows, 195 blocks, grid 1.29 (offset 0,1.6) → 97 whites in 98 nominal slots (99%), 4 free slots at start.
- Grid dots are drawn on every cell an arrow may occupy (editor clearance rule); artist-conveyor levels only.

## Rules
- Start at 99% (whites = round(capacity × .99)). Fail = belt ≥100% and no front cannon can clear a block.
- An arrow longer than the free belt room is refused as `'full'` (meter flash), never jams into a fail.
- Red vignette pulses from the start; it hides below 95%.
- Hint: arrows the front cannons absorb exactly; otherwise any free arrow that still leaves 8 slots (`hintReserve`).

## Motion (`preloadTuning` in `src/core/simulation.ts`)
- Belt `blocksPerSecond: 10` (shipped game). Arrow flight `launchFactor: 1.6` × belt, easing to belt speed at the join.
- Entry fix: the arrow flies straight until it comes within `mergeCells` (.9 cell) of the belt centerline, then a
  quadratic merge curve lands `mergeShift` (.75 unit) downstream, aligned with the belt. Counter-flowing belts:
  land straight (no U-turn). Head turn rate capped (`turnRate` 20 rad/s). The head waits at the belt edge until
  the gap is clear; upstream blocks hold behind every belt point the curve passes near.
  Root cause before: 49 of 59 arrows hit the belt slanted, on a bend, or ran parallel to its edge.
- Blocks stay flat on the board and climb only in their last `riseCells` (1.4) before the belt (`arrowStyle`).
- Belt pitch margin .015 (the 1.29 grid's bends overlapped at .01).

## Cannons
- Queues generated by `node scripts/plan-cannons.mjs` → `src/data/overloadCannons.ts`. Ramp: small openers
  (`openAmmo` 2, `ramp` 1, `maxAmmo` 36), lanes by least load, white cannons from the 3rd slot on, spread for
  gradual draining; lane totals balanced with white ammo; replay check (hint player, 180 s, no fail/stall).
- Current whites left every 15 s (hint player): 97, 97, 78, 42, 25, 13, 0 — full board clear.
- Shot flight .06 s. Hit VFX: colored shards, white sparks, flash, muzzle flash (`burstTuning`).
- Emptied cannon swells, shrinks and bursts (`retireTuning`); an empty lane rolls a door down (`doorTuning`).
  These two animations were not yet observed in a browser.
- Lanes render 1.35× (`arrowStyle.laneScale`) about the muzzle; the core detector is unchanged.

## Builds and analytics (2026-10-29)
- `build:all` ships this level in four lengths: `3clicks`, `10clicks`, `60seconds`, `full` (8 networks each).
  A click is any tap on an arrow (launched, blocked or refused). Packages are about 3.66 MB, under the 4 MB cap.
- AppLovin analytics: LOADING, LOADED, DISPLAYED, CHALLENGE_STARTED, CHALLENGE_PASS_25/50/75 (share of all blocks
  cleared), CHALLENGE_SOLVED, CHALLENGE_FAILED (belt overload only), ENDCARD_SHOWN, CTA_CLICKED; each sent once.
  No CHALLENGE_RETRY: the playable has no retry.
